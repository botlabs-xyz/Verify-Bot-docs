## FAQ

### Is VerifyBot public?

VerifyBot is documented publicly, but project policy notes it as a private bot project.

### Can I self-host VerifyBot?

Current project notice indicates self-hosting is not supported.

### Why do verifications fail even with admin permissions?

Most failures are role hierarchy related. Ensure VerifyBot role is above target roles.

### What is the fastest way to validate setup?

Run this sequence:
1. `/addverifyrole`
2. `/setlogs`
3. `/verify` on a test account
